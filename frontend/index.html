Magnetic Confinement Fusion Milestones (2025–2030)
Magnetic confinement fusion is transitioning from scientific feasibility demonstrations to pilot plant construction. CFS and MIT have confirmed SPARC assembly targets for net energy gain (Q > 1) by late 2026 using 20-Tesla HTS magnets [1]. Tokamak Energy demonstrated 100M°C plasma confinement in spherical tokamaks and is targeting grid-connected pilot operations by 2033 [2]. Meanwhile, the revised ITER baseline targets first plasma with full magnetic coil validation in 2034 [3].
